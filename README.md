## 💡 Core Insight

**The research is brilliant. The prototype works. The concept is sound.**

**BUT:** It's written for a different context (funded team, not solo dev).

**Reality:** As a solo developer with AI help and low budget:
- ✅ You CAN build a working version (85% probability)
- ✅ You WILL learn valuable skills (95% probability)
- ⚠️ You MIGHT get traction (10-15% probability)
- ❌ You PROBABLY WON'T make serious money Year 1 (5-10% probability)

**Build this if:**
- You want to learn Web Audio API, game physics, canvas optimization
- You're okay with $0-1,000 revenue Year 1
- You enjoy the building process itself
- You can commit 7-11 months

**Don't build this if:**
- You need income within 6 months
- You're expecting viral success
- You can't handle 90% chance of "failure"
- You have other higher-priority projects
