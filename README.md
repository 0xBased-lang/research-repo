## 📁 Current Research Projects

### 1. Customer App for Passive Income (November 2025)
**Branch:** `claude/research-customer-app-019ZeYTPHPV6KDC4sY2zq8PG`

Comprehensive research on building customer-facing applications with passive income potential. Covers market analysis, technical architecture, monetization strategies, and complete action plan.

**Documents:**
- **CUSTOMER_APP_RESEARCH.md** - Main 60+ page research report
- **QUICK_REFERENCE_GUIDE.md** - One-page cheat sheet for quick decisions
- **APP_IDEA_EVALUATION_TEMPLATE.md** - Framework for scoring app ideas
- **APP_IDEAS_BRAINSTORM_2025.md** - 15 detailed app ideas with full analysis
- **IDEA_COMPARISON_MATRIX.md** - Quick comparison matrix and decision filters
- **PERSONA_BASED_APP_IDEAS.md** - 20 persona-driven ideas solving intersecting challenges
- **ADVANCED_CATEGORIZATION_FRAMEWORK.md** - 8-dimensional idea organization system

**Status:** ✅ Research Complete | ✅ Brainstorming Complete | ✅ Persona Analysis Complete | ✅ Advanced Framework Complete

**Quick Start:**
1. Read `QUICK_REFERENCE_GUIDE.md` for overview (10 min)
2. Review `PERSONA_BASED_APP_IDEAS.md` for persona-driven ideas (45 min) **[NEW!]**
3. Use `ADVANCED_CATEGORIZATION_FRAMEWORK.md` to filter by YOUR criteria (30 min) **[NEW!]**
4. Compare finalists in `IDEA_COMPARISON_MATRIX.md` (15 min)
5. Score top 3 with `APP_IDEA_EVALUATION_TEMPLATE.md` (30 min each)
6. Reference `CUSTOMER_APP_RESEARCH.md` for implementation details

**Key Findings:**
- Mobile-first is mandatory (66% of traffic)
- Micro-SaaS models enable passive income with minimal overhead
- Onboarding quality determines 76% retention
- No-code platforms viable for rapid validation
- Target <5% monthly churn for sustainable passive income
