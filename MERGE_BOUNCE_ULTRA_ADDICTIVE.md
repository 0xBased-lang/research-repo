## 🧠 THE ADDICTION SCIENCE FOUNDATION

### Psychological Mechanisms We're Exploiting

Based on comprehensive research into Peggle, Plinko, 2048, Suika Game, and Ballz, here are the core dopamine triggers:

#### 1. **Variable Reward Schedule** (Plinko Psychology)
- **The Hook:** Each bounce is unpredictable → brain releases dopamine in anticipation
- **In Merge Bounce:** Every shot has potential for massive combos OR disappointing misses
- **Why It Works:** Same mechanism as slot machines - variable rewards are MORE addictive than guaranteed rewards

#### 2. **Flow State + Near Misses** (Peggle's Genius)
- **The Hook:** "Almost made it!" feeling keeps players trying
- **In Merge Bounce:** Ball bounces RIGHT NEXT to matching number but doesn't touch
- **Why It Works:** Near misses activate same brain regions as wins, but create frustration that demands resolution

#### 3. **Progression Dopamine** (2048/Merge Games)
- **The Hook:** Watching numbers grow: 2→4→8→16→32→64→128→256→512→1024→2048→4096
- **In Merge Bounce:** Each merge is visual proof of progress
- **Why It Works:** Number doubling creates exponential satisfaction

#### 4. **Cascade Euphoria** (Match-3 Psychology)
- **The Hook:** Chain reactions trigger multiple dopamine hits in rapid succession
- **In Merge Bounce:** One ball creates 2-3-4-5 consecutive merges
- **Why It Works:** Each merge = dopamine hit. 5 merges in 2 seconds = dopamine FLOOD

#### 5. **Skill + Chaos Balance** (Ballz Formula)
- **The Hook:** You AIM (skill) but physics creates chaos (luck)
- **In Merge Bounce:** Strategic targeting meets unpredictable bouncing
- **Why It Works:** Players credit wins to skill, blame losses on bad luck
