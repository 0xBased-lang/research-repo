**18-22.25 points: STRONG GO** ✅

- Prioritize this idea
- Move to prototype phase

**14-17.99 points: CAUTIOUS GO** ⚠️
- Has potential but with concerns
- Address weaknesses before committing
- Consider as backup option

**10-13.99 points: NEEDS IMPROVEMENT** 🔄
- Significant gaps or risks
- Pivot the idea or find different angle
- Not ready for development

**<10 points: PASS** ❌
- Low probability of success
- Move on to different idea
- Revisit only if circumstances change dramatically
