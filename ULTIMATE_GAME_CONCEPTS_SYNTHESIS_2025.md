**LAYER 2: Quantum Superposition**
```

  Traditional: 2+2 = 4 (boring)

  Quantum: 2+2 = [4|8|16|32] (superposition!)

Ball exists in MULTIPLE STATES until "observed"
Click to collapse wavefunction:
  - 70% chance: 4 (expected)
  - 20% chance: 8 (double!)
  - 8% chance: 16 (quadruple!)
  - 2% chance: 32+ (JACKPOT!)

STRATEGY: When to observe?
  - Wait for more merges = higher probabilities
  - But board fills up = risk
```

**LAYER 3: Gravity Flip (Your Favorite!)**
```
- Tap to flip gravity (up/down)
- Balls fly upward, create new merge opportunities
- Pegs work both directions
- Strategic: "I'll flip to make THESE balls merge!"
```

**LAYER 4: Combo Chain Reactions**
```
One merge triggers nearby merges
Quantum states collapse in sequence
Creates cascading dopamine hits

Example:
  Merge 1: 2+2 → Collapses to 8 (lucky!)
  Merge 2: 8 touches another 8 → 16!
  Merge 3: 16's energy field triggers nearby 4+4 → 8
  Merge 4: Chain continues...

Result: "MEGA COMBO x7!" + explosion effects
```

**LAYER 5: Probability Manipulation**
```
POWER-UPS (earned through gameplay):

🔬 "Observation Delay"
  - Keep ball in superposition longer
  - Each nearby merge boosts probability
  - Risk: Board fills up

🎯 "Probability Boost"
  - Next collapse has +30% better outcome
  - Limited uses (3 per game)

🌀 "Entanglement"
  - Link 2 quantum balls
  - When one collapses, other gets same bonus
  - Creates massive combos

⏮️ "Rewind"
  - Don't like outcome? Rewind once
  - Ball returns to superposition
  - Different roll now
```
